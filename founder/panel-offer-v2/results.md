## What would flip a no

- If after the 14 days my own record clearly showed I fall asleep something like 15+ minutes faster than with YouTube, I might pay the yearly plan.
- If the day-14 record clearly showed I fall asleep 10+ minutes faster than with my YouTube routine, and it were a one-time payment of around 10,000 won instead of a subscription.
- If my own day-14 record showed I got back to sleep after waking at 3-4am clearly faster, like 15+ minutes, I'd probably pay the 4,900 won for a month to see if it holds up.
- If the 14-day record honestly showed I fell asleep noticeably faster (like 10+ minutes) without me having to do the check-in every morning, and the price was closer to 2,000-3,000 won a month.
- If after 14 days my record showed a big drop, like falling asleep 20+ minutes faster, and there was a cheap one-time or student price around 2,000 won a month.
- If my day-14 record showed a big, obvious drop, like falling asleep 15+ minutes faster, I might pay for one month to see if it holds.
- If my day-14 record showed I was clearly falling asleep a lot faster, like 15+ minutes, I might pay for one month just to see if it holds.
- If my own 14-day record clearly showed I fell asleep 15+ minutes faster than with YouTube, I might pay the yearly plan, but only if it worked that well without a watch.
- If my 14-day record clearly showed I fell back asleep faster after the 3-4am wake-ups, and the app store had a few thousand reviews around 4.5 stars saying the same thing.
- If the day-14 record actually showed me falling asleep 10+ minutes faster, even on my rotating shift nights, I'd pay the 4,900.
- If my own 14-day record showed I was actually falling asleep a lot faster, like 15+ minutes quicker than with YouTube, I might pay for the yearly plan.
- A solid app-store rating (4.5+ with a few thousand reviews) or a real study showing people fall asleep faster, plus my own 14-day record showing a clear drop of 10+ minutes.
