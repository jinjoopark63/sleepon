# SLEEP:ON (스르르): competitors sold in Korea

Checked: 2026-11-25. Public web sources only.

## Read this first: how the data was collected

- **I could not open the stores directly.** In this session `apps.apple.com`, `play.google.com`, and every other site I tried (official sites, apkpure, etc.) failed DNS resolution through the network sandbox. To fetch store pages directly, those hosts have to be allowed in the session's network settings.
- **Every fact below comes from search-engine snippets of the cited pages.** The snippets are cached copies with **unknown capture dates**. Several apps show different ratings and prices in different snapshots. Where that happens, I list all of them.
- **I could not sort reviews by star rating.** Snippets rarely show a review's star count. So the review themes below are "complaints that surfaced", not a true 1-3 star sample. Counts are rough, and most themes are **thin** (under 3 reviews).
- **Quotes are research, not testimonials.** Quotes are verbatim where the snippet gave verbatim text. Paraphrases are marked *(paraphrase)*.
- **Rankings are not verified.** I could not see live KR chart positions. These apps were picked because they come up repeatedly for 수면 앱 / 백색소음 / 수면 명상 searches.

## Takeaways for SLEEP:ON

1. **Price:** SLEEP:ON's ₩4,900 / ₩39,000 is the cheapest subscription in this set. Renight costs about 1.6x monthly and 2x yearly.
2. **Positioning gap:** I found no Korean app that publicly claims "stop when you fall asleep, resume when you wake". That combination looks open, based on public pages only.
3. **Main competitor:** Renight is the closest. It is Korean, AI-generated/personalised sound, and heavily marketed. Its public weak spots are a paywall-first model with trial/renewal friction, UI churn after renames, and mid-night waking.
4. **Technical threat:** Asleep already sells mic-based sleep-onset auto-stop as an SDK. Renight or anyone else could add it quickly, so the moat has to be the learned fade/resume behaviour and the wearable, not detection alone.
5. **Shared risk:** paywall-change backlash (슬립루틴) and payment/membership errors (코끼리) recur. Keep the free tier stable and the renewal terms clear.
